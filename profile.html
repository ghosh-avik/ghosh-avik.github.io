About

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in UC San Diego Electrical and Computer Engineering, working with Prof. Yuanyuan Shi and Prof. Sujit Dey.

I received my PhD from the Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering department at UC San Diego in 2025, jointly advised by Prof. Jan Kleissl and Prof. Sonia Martínez. During the summer of 2023 I was a PhD intern at Pacific Northwest National Laboratory, working with Dr. Veronica Adetola.

Before UC San Diego I completed a Bachelor of Technology in Mechanical Engineering at the Indian Institute of Engineering Science and Technology, Shibpur in 2019, where I worked with Prof. Aritra Ganguly. I spent the summer of 2018 at the Indian Institute of Technology, Kanpur with Prof. Jishnu Bhattacharya.
